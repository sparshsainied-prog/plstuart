Nightsoul. A name striking terror into the hearts of Eltnia's people for decades.

Othrun once killed Nightsoul's mother, Nulthe, at her own coven. Now Borenia's shadowy monarch has moved against Othrun's father-in-law, King Irly of Badonia, and Othrun has to march west into the gloom with his wife, the mage Sila, at his side.

Read it in order

Othrun begins as a flawed and bigoted lord. The saga follows him towards change, and towards power in a new world after the fall of his homeland, which is based on Plato's lost Atlantis.

P.L. Stuart

P.L. was born in Toronto and holds a university degree in English, specialising in Medieval Literature. He is an Assistant Editor for Before We Go Blog and one of the hosts of Page Chewing.

On his own YouTube channel he hosts Six Elementals Interviews, live on Saturdays, chatting with fantasy and science fiction authors and readers.
